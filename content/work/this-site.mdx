## Why build it this way

A portfolio about AI and design systems should show both, not describe them. So the site is the demo.

## How it works

- **Next.js on Vercel**, statically generated, with Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights.
- **Keystatic** for editing. Articles and case studies are MDX files in GitHub; the editor at `/keystatic` commits to the repository. Live demos are React components editors drop into any article.
- **An in-browser model.** The Ask guide and the [playground](/playground) run a small language model with WebLLM and WebGPU. Questions never leave your device and there's no server bill.
- **Built for agents too.** Every page has structured data, and [`/llms.txt`](/llms.txt) gives AI agents a plain-text map of the site.
