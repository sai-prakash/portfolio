## The problem

Positioning cash is careful, repetitive work. Before Liquidity Co-Pilot, one recurring liquidity task took about 218 minutes of a specialist's day, most of it spent gathering and reconciling information rather than making the decision itself.

## What we built

An assistant that does the gathering and drafting, and leaves the decision with the person. The design principle was simple: the model prepares, the human approves. Every suggestion shows where it came from, so a specialist can check it in seconds instead of redoing it.

<BeforeAfter label="Time for the liquidity task" before={218} after={37} unit="min" />

## Result

The task now takes about 37 minutes, an 83% reduction, and adoption among the team that does it has been high.

## What I'd tell another team

- Measure the task before you build. A before number you trust is what turns a demo into a business case.
- Design the review step as carefully as the generation step. That's where trust is won or lost.
- Ship to the people who do the work first, then widen.
