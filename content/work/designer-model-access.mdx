## The problem

Designers who wanted to try LLMs had to wait for engineering. Every experiment became a ticket, and most ideas died in the queue.

## The move

An SDK, a playground and an API server that put the firm's internal models directly in designers' hands. On top of that, the MDS AI Assist Figma plugin, and MCP servers so agents can reach the same capabilities.

## Result

Designers try models on their own now. The AI Assist Figma plugin reached more than 400 opens a month.

## My part

I built the SDK, the playground and the API server. I own the plugin's UX and design research, including how its findings and scores are presented, after handing its codebase to engineering. I also ran the MCP audit and wrote the spec.

## What I learned

A score is only useful if people trust it and know what to do next. The design work was making each finding specific, explaining why it matters and showing the fix, rather than handing people a number.
