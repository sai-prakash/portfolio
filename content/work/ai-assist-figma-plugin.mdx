## What it is

A Figma plugin that checks a designer's work against the Manhattan Design System and suggests fixes. Engineering owns the codebase; I own the experience: what the plugin says, how its score is shown, and the research behind both.

## The design problem

A score is only useful if designers trust it and know what to do next. The work was about making each finding specific, explaining why it matters, and showing the fix, rather than handing people a number.

## Result

The plugin is opened more than 400 times a month.
